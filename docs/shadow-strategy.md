# Frozen strategy specification

Instrument: Bybit BTCUSD inverse perpetual. Entry decisions occur only on a fully finalized UTC 5-minute grid. Frozen Direction `UP`→LONG or `DOWN`→SHORT requires the unchanged actionable confidence ≥0.55. While a position is open, the same frozen model monitors the completed 1-minute bars at `t+1` through `t+4`; an opposite actionable result causes `DIRECTION_FLIP` at the next causal public execution observation. Otherwise the 5-minute `HORIZON` exit occurs at `t+5`. At a tied horizon/entry boundary, the old position exits before an actionable new entry. No intrabar TP/SL or off-grid re-entry.

One position maximum. Isolated margin is 20% of current equity, target notional 1.8 times that margin. Entry and exit each incur 5.5 bp taker fee and 2 bp adverse slippage. No stop-loss, averaging, pyramiding, or real order. The original historical ledger assumes next-open/close prices with zero additional latency. The forward driver intentionally waits for a public trade whose exchange and receive timestamps are both **after** inference readiness; that is a causal execution observation, not proof of historical fill-price parity. Latency and its effect must be audited before readiness.

The pure engine and decision driver are not a deployed persistent service. Inverse contract quantity rounding, liquidation tier semantics, DB-backed restart restoration under `bybit_shadow`, and end-to-end latency remain unverified. No rule is to be inferred from backtest outcomes.
